iWhat is gross debt?

Gross debt is the full currency amount recorded against a position when it mints. The retained reserve and the upfront fee are part of this gross figure, so the amount that arrives in a wallet is always lower than the gross amount.

Read the borrowing costs guide
iWhat is the retained reserve?

A share of each mint that stays assigned to the position instead of arriving in the wallet. It backs the currency over time and later reduces the currency needed for full repayment.

Read the reserves and equity guide
iWhat is the upfront fee?

A one-time fee taken from the gross mint when currency is created. It is separate from the retained reserve and from ongoing interest.

People propose, veto, challenge and bid. Understanding their roles is the next step.

◇Overcollateralized
Why collateral and debt are different quantities

A position is designed to secure its currency debt with collateral. Collateral value can change; the ratio in our model is a teaching assumption, not a guarantee or a live eligibility check.

Read the collateral and debt guide
◎Oracle-free
How participants contest a stored price

Participants can challenge a position and take part in the auction process. This replaces an external liquidation price oracle with an economic mechanism that depends on people acting.

⌘Decentralized participation
Who proposes, who vetoes, who operates

Borrowers propose positions. Qualified governance participants can veto within the applicable window. Challengers and bidders take part under contract rules. V2 deployment choices determine the complete trust model.

Does depositing a token automatically allow minting?

No. A new position follows its proposal and veto process, and minting remains subject to its terms and on-chain state. The frontend does not approve collateral.

Can we deposit and provide liquidity with the same tokens?

Tokens locked in a position are collateral. A DEX pair needs a separate token allocation alongside the currency you mint.

Who assesses a project’s collateral?

Protocol participants assess proposals and can exercise veto rights where qualified. The interface presents information and actions; it does not certify projects or DEX liquidity.

Is the workspace connected to live contracts?

The app includes read-only V2 tooling, but no verified V2 deployment registry is currently configured. You can model a position, save a draft on this device and export a plan. It does not simulate, sign or broadcast transactions.
