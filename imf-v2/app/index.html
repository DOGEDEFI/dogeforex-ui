A plan stops before authority begins.

Human and agent workflows share inputs and evidence. Signing policy and transaction execution are outside this integration.

  1. 1
    Plan and identify

    Choose new terms, accepted clone terms or an existing position. Identify the currency, chain and actual V2 contracts.

  2. 2
    Verify current reads

    Read bytecode, contract links, token metadata, terms, funding, capacity and time at a source block. Failure is not zero.

  3. 3
    Review read-derived quotes

    Separate gross debt, reserve, fees, spendable proceeds, repayment and collateral effects. Requote mutable state before any action.

  4. 4
    Separate simulation and treasury review

    A later authorized integration must simulate the exact method and present spender, recipient and expected changes. This frontend does not do so.

  5. 5
    Separate DEX and execution decisions

    Use a separate token allocation for DEX liquidity. Verify venue, pool, LP ownership and signing policy independently. Exported plans authorize none of these.
