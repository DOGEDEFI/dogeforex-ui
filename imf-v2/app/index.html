Challenges and auctions, with the clock visible.
First comes the aversion window, then the Dutch auction. Expired collateral purchases use a different price curve.

      

      

        
Start a challenge
You supply the challenged collateral. The minimum-price guard protects against a lower price being inserted before your transaction.

          

        

        
Bid or cancel
In the aversion window, a non-challenger buys collateral at the liquidation price; the challenger can cancel. In the auction, price declines with time.

          

        

      

      

        
Buy expired collateral
Only expired positions without an outstanding challenge. The current price and total currency cost are reread before simulation.

          

        

        
Withdraw postponed collateral
For a challenger with a pending return. The entire pending amount goes to the selected recipient.

Authority stays with the wallet holder.
Human and agent workflows share planning inputs and evidence. Only an explicit wallet review can become a DogeOS testnet transaction.

      
  1. 1
    Choose the journey
    Create or clone, mint, manage or close, challenge or bid, bridge, save, invest or participate in governance. Each currency remains an independent system.
  2. 2
    Connect and verify current reads
    Connect MetaMask, switch to DogeOS, and verify the registry, chain, contract links, token metadata, balances and timers. Failure is not zero.
  3. 3
    Review terms and exact approvals
    Separate gross debt, reserve, fees, proceeds, repayment and collateral effects. Any required approval uses an exact amount and a separate confirmation.
  4. 4
    Simulate and confirm once
    The app checks the exact caller, target and calldata with a fresh static call and gas estimate. Review the full transaction in the app and then in MetaMask. No follow-up transaction is automatic.
  5. 5
    Reconcile the receipt
    A pending hash is not a success or a reason to resubmit. Confirmation refreshes current state; a reverted transaction stays reverted. DEX liquidity, keeper automation and agent-originated execution remain separate decisions.
