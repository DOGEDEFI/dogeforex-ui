iStep 1, current terms: what happens?

The app rereads the position's live terms — collateral, price, capacity, cooldowns and caller — before preparing anything. Stale or failed reads stop the journey instead of guessing.

Read the borrowing costs guide
iStep 2, simulation: what is checked?

A fresh static call runs the exact transaction against current state and a gas estimate is added. Simulation reflects the chain as it is now, not a future guarantee.

Read the system architecture guide
iStep 3, review: what do I check?

You see the exact call, required approvals and expected effects in the app first. Nothing is signed at this stage; planning documents and quotes carry no authority on their own.

Read the agent workflows guide
iStep 4, confirmation and receipt: what happens?

The wallet shows the final transaction and network fee. One explicit confirmation submits it; the receipt is journaled and is never resubmitted or extended automatically.

Data & methodology

        

          
Verified reads, not generated telemetry. Dashboard values come from all 12 independently verified runtime-config V2 registries. The isolated fixture is explicitly labelled synthetic and never represents deployed state.

          
Hourly dashboard cache
Public dashboard analytics are cached for up to one hour with the exact registry, chain, RPC and runtime identity. The snapshot shows its update age. Expired entries remain labelled while refreshing; Reload now forces fresh verification and reads. Confirmed receipts invalidate cached analytics. Quotes, eligibility, simulation, gas estimation and every guided transaction path always use fresh state.

          
Collateral ratio and liquidation
Dashboard CR uses collateral balance × the position’s stored liquidation price ÷ gross minted debt. It is a comparative contract-parameter ratio, not an external market valuation. Each position has its own liquidation boundary; the Risk Lab threshold is only a user watch setting.

          
Equity, reserves and bridge capacity
System equity, minter reserve, savings, token supply and bridge accounting are separate current reads. They are not interchangeable assets, guaranteed loss coverage, FX backing or a shared cross-currency reserve.

          
Discovery and provenance
Events identify positions and challenges; current contract calls verify their state. Every snapshot retains its registry, source block and coverage. Failed, missing or stale reads remain unknown rather than zero.

          
Unavailable analytics
Historical charts, volume, complete event history, reference marks and market depth require an indexer or verified market feed. Reference marks would be analytics inputs, never protocol oracle prices or proof of executable liquidity.

iWhat is the liquidation price?
The price, in local currency per whole collateral token, below which the position is challengeable. Participants contest a stored price through challenges instead of relying on an external price oracle.
Read the collateral proposals guide

                

                

                  
                  
                

                

                
iWhat is the minting maximum?
The gross debt ceiling a position may record over its lifetime. Minting stops once the position reaches this maximum, regardless of how much collateral remains.
Read the collateral proposals guide

                

                  

                  
iWhat is the initialization period?
The waiting time after a position is proposed before minting can begin. Qualified participants can veto the position only during this window; once it ends, the position runs under its recorded terms.
Read the participants and vetoes guide

                  
                

                

                  
                  

                  
iWhat is the risk premium (PPM)?
An extra annual interest charge on a position, expressed in parts per million. 10,000 PPM equals 1% per year, charged on top of the savings rate while debt is outstanding.
Read the borrowing costs guide

                

                

                
iWhat is the reserve contribution (PPM)?
The share of each mint assigned to the retained reserve, expressed in parts per million. 200,000 PPM means 20% of the gross mint stays reserved.
Read the borrowing costs guide

Start a challenge
You supply the challenged collateral. The minimum-price guard protects against a lower price being inserted before your transaction.

          

        

        
Bid or cancel
In the aversion window, a non-challenger buys collateral at the liquidation price; the challenger can cancel. In the auction, price declines with time.
